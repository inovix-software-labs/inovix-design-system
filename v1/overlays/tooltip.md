## 5. Behavior + interaction rules (keyboard, mouse, touch, dismiss, debounce, drag, long-press, right-click)

- Keyboard: tooltip shows on trigger focus after delay, hides on blur, Escape hides.
- Mouse: hover trigger shows after --duration-tooltip-delay, hides on leave, click does not dismiss.
- Touch: long-press shows where declared, tap elsewhere hides, no hover on coarse pointers.
- Dismiss: pointer leave + Escape + blur, tooltip is transient.
- Offline: tip text cached, shown instantly.
- Debounce: show and hide debounce --duration-tooltip-delay.

## 11. Do / Do not list

Do: reference tokens; show on focus and hover; keep tip short; flip in viewport; hold 40% expansion.
Do not: hardcode width; place tip over trigger; hide ring on trigger; put interactive content in tip; autoplay sound.
Premium treatment: premium tip fade via --ease-standard, crisp arrow, no layout shift on show.

## 12. Accessibility requirements (role, aria, focus, contrast, screen reader, reduced motion, high contrast, forced colors)

Per-part aria mapping:
- container: role=tooltip, aria-hidden until shown
- body: id referenced by aria-describedby on trigger
- trigger: aria-describedby points to tip body

Focus: :focus-visible ring --shadow-focus-glow + 2 units --color-border-focus on trigger, never outline:none.
Contrast: AA 4.5:1 tip text on inverse both themes; focus ring 3:1; --color-text-primary on --color-surface-inverse verified.

## 13. i18n requirements (text expansion, bidi, script, plural, locale format)

- Tip text: externalized, 40% expansion, ellipsis policy, no mid-word break.
- Show delay per locale reading speed token.
- Bidi isolate spans; RTL mirrors arrow and placement.

All strings externalized to locale files; zero literals in code. Layouts hold 40% expansion from en without truncation, overlap, or fixed heights. Bidi: Unicode algorithm, isolate spans, no concatenated mixed-direction strings. Scripts: Latin/Cyrillic/Greek first with CJK/Devanagari/Arabic fallback stacks from --font-ui; per-script line-height via --leading-* tokens, never fixed px. Plurals: CLDR categories with count token, never hardcoded suffix. Formats: date, time, number, currency, calendar, first-day-of-week, timezone all per locale token; store UTC, label always shown. RTL: full mirror per section 9. Truncation: ellipsis token policy only, never mid-word, tooltip on truncated. Hyphenation: off unless token enables per locale. Line breaking: CJK/Thai rules, no overflow hidden on text.
