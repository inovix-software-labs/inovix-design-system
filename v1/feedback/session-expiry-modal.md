## 5. Behavior + interaction rules (keyboard, mouse, touch, dismiss, debounce, drag, long-press, right-click)

- Keyboard: focus trapped in panel; Tab cycles extend/leave; Enter activates; Escape does not dismiss (expiry is blocking); countdown announced once.
- Mouse: hover washes actions; click activates; scrim click does nothing.
- Touch: actions >= --touch-target-min; tap activates; scrim tap does nothing.
- Dismiss: only extend or leave; no outside-click dismiss; no Escape dismiss.
- Debounce: extend debounces --duration-debounce-input; double-extend blocked.
- Offline: countdown local; extend queues; leave navigates to cached page.
