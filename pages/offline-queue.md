## 5. Behavior + interaction rules (keyboard, mouse, touch, dismiss, debounce, drag, long-press, right-click)

- Keyboard: Tab reaches actions; Enter replays or discards; rows are status; announce replay result.
- Mouse: hover washes actions; click replays/discards; discard confirms via popover.
- Touch: actions >= --touch-target-min; tap activates; confirm popover edge-flips.
- Dismiss: discard confirm closes Escape/outside; focus returns.
- Debounce: replay debounces --duration-debounce-input; double-replay blocked.
- Offline: queue renders from cache; replay waits for reconnect; status shows queued.
